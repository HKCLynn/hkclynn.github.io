I am a **Ph.D. student** at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. [Ying-Cong Chen](https://www.yingcong.me/).

My research interests include **3D reconstruction, generative models, and embodied AI**.

I received my M.Phil. from HKUST(GZ) in 2026 and completed my undergraduate studies at South China University of Technology in 2024.
